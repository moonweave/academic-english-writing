# Curated paper patterns

Seven papers, fourteen passage-level patterns. Inspected 2026-10-23. Full source text was accessible; only the named passages were closely analyzed. This is not a complete reading or scientific validation of every paper. Selections are editorial judgments, not an external ranking or user endorsement.

Read only the relevant domain file. Each entry records two specific locations, the writing decisions they illustrate, and limits on transfer. No original paper sentences are reproduced. Do not treat the findings as evidence for a different manuscript.

| File | Papers | Patterns | Choose for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Materials](papers-materials.md) | 2 | 4 | Quantitative motivation, controlled comparisons, observation-to-model transitions |
| [Polymers](papers-polymers.md) | 2 | 4 | Conflicting literature, trade-offs, temporal evolution, measurement artifacts |

The hydrogel paper also covers polymer writing; graphene spans materials and electronics. The electronics set deliberately spans devices, circuit reviews, and communication theory. It does not yet provide equally strong coverage of RF measurement or ADC implementation papers.
