# Curated paper patterns

Eleven papers, twenty-one operational patterns, inspected 2026-10-23. Eight core sources supply fourteen patterns; three auxiliary sources supply seven. The core combines documented research influence or attention with a separate editorial assessment of named prose passages. These are provisional curator selections, not user-endorsed favorites or an external best-writing ranking.

| File | Core sources / patterns | Auxiliary sources / patterns | Choose for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Materials](papers-materials.md) | M1, M2, M3 / 5 | None | Concession; mechanisms; requirements; observation before model; material-state definitions |
| [Polymers](papers-polymers.md) | P1, P4 / 3 | P2, P3 / 5 | Core: literature conflict, trade-offs, conditional stability. Auxiliary: time evolution, checks, thermal procedure, synthesis, characterization |
| [Electronics](papers-electronics.md) | E1, E2, E3 / 6 | E4 / 2 | Core: operating conditions, interventions, functions, comparison, theory. Auxiliary: ADC sweeps and power accounting |

Choose one or two patterns matching the rhetorical problem. Prefer core entries when equally suitable; use auxiliary entries for a specific uncovered need. Auxiliary does not mean scientifically inferior: their attention credentials were not established in this curation, and some passages model argument only. Do not imitate their awkward collocations. Do not force a core abstract pattern onto Methods or Results merely because the paper is prominent.

M3 and P4 were inspected at abstract level only. The other nine sources had full source text accessible during earlier curation, with named passages closely analyzed; this does not mean every page was reviewed. Source versions, locators, short checked fragments, editorial schematics, original invented applications and transfer limits are in the domain files. Invented examples never provide manuscript facts.

[Selection rationale](selection-rationale.md) records the two selection axes, dated evidence, access limits and exclusions. [Worked examples](worked-examples.md) demonstrate editing choices. [Comparative evaluation](../evals/protocol.md) tests writing separately from corpus credentials; the existing comparisons do not establish overall superiority.
