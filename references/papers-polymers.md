# Polymers: three core and five auxiliary patterns

Editorial analyses inspected 2026-10-23. Short quotations identify source wording; ellipses are not used to reconstruct long passages. Grammatical analyses and abstract schemata are editorial interpretations. Every application sentence below is an original invented teaching example, not a source finding or a reusable data claim. Choose a pattern because it serves the argument; do not insert every pattern or copy a source’s distinctive wording.

## P1 — core source

Marc Guerre et al. (2020), “Vitrimers: directing chemical reactivity to control material properties,” Chemical Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1039/D0SC01069C); [full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8159211/). Europe PMC XML inspected. Minireview: distinguish cited findings from the authors’ experiments.

### P1a: Compare findings before explaining disagreement

**Locator:** Cross-link density of vitrimer matrices, comparison paragraph. **Source fragment:** “the opposite phenomenon was reported”.

**Structure (editorial schematic, not a quotation):** [reported trend in one study] -> [contrast] -> [opposite result subject] + [was reported]. A later [may + explanation] clause belongs to interpretation, not the report itself.

**How it works:** An attribution passive names a literature observation; contrast then separates it from the preceding report. An interpretive clause should be attributed or hedged independently. Preserve who found what instead of merging different studies into one general law.

**Original application:** Study A reported slower relaxation at higher crosslink density, whereas Study B found the reverse trend. Different exchange chemistries may contribute to this difference.

**Limits:** Study A/B are invented labels, not citations. Supply real references and do not assert a resolved cause without discriminating evidence.

### P1b: Keep a recommendation beside its cost

**Locator:** Partial irreversible cross-linking of the vitrimer matrix. **Source fragment:** “However, the strategy was not equally effective”.

**Structure (editorial schematic, not a quotation):** [benefit] -> [However + definite intervention subject + was not + effectiveness complement + scope]. Cost or range limits stay adjacent to the recommendation.

**How it works:** However opens a scope restriction after a benefit. The strategy is a definite reference to an already introduced intervention. Follow with the range or cost that determines where the recommendation holds, rather than a generic final caveat.

**Original application:** Adding permanent junctions reduced creep, although it also slowed stress relaxation. The formulation therefore favors dimensional stability over rapid reshaping.

**Limits:** The trade-off and application priority must be supported. Do not convert a relative benefit into a universal recommendation.

## P2 — auxiliary source

Botuo Zheng et al. (2024), “Structural evolution during inverse vulcanization,” Nature Communications. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-49374-y); [full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11217493/). Europe PMC XML inspected. Local argument and method examples; not a blanket grammar model.

### P2a: Describe time evolution without compressing distinct stages

**Locator:** Inverse vulcanization curves of crosslinker monomers, Fig. 2a. **Source fragment:** “As the reaction proceeded”.

**Structure (editorial schematic, not a quotation):** [As + process subject + past verb], [observable subject + past verb + trend]. A later interpretation is a separate assertion; temporal order alone does not supply its cause.

**How it works:** A temporal subordinate clause locates an observation without asserting its cause. Keep temporal order distinct from the subsequent inference about network structure. Past tense reports the recorded series; present can describe the plotted curve.

**Original application:** As irradiation continued, the viscosity first increased and then approached a plateau. This sequence is consistent with a slowing structural change.

**Limits:** Do not rename a plateau “complete conversion” without conversion measurements. This source models local argument structure; its awkward collocations are not language targets.

### P2b: Use a purpose clause to motivate a check

**Locator:** same subsection, complementary frequency-sweep comparison. **Source fragment:** “Excluding the potential cavities through hot-pressing”.

**Structure (editorial schematic, not a quotation):** Source construction: [fronted -ing clause describing removal of a possible artifact], [results subject + inference verb + conclusion + qualification]. Optional recast: [To assess alternative], [we performed check]; [bounded result]. This recast is an editorial alternative, not source wording.

**How it works:** A purpose/check/result sequence connects the alternative explanation to the next measurement. The source uses a fronted participial phrase; in a new manuscript, a non-finite purpose clause introduced by to + infinitive can state the check’s purpose, with its understood subject supplied by the explicit main-clause subject (we). This makes agency and logical scope clearer without implying that an artifact was fully excluded.

**Original application:** To assess a possible edge-drying artifact, we repeated the measurement with a sealed edge. The trend persisted, although the late-time values differed.

**Limits:** Only describe a completed check if it was performed. Persistence of a trend does not exclude every artifact; retain discrepant conditions.

### P2c: Preserve the procedure that defines an extracted property

**Locator:** Methods > Characterization > Differential scanning calorimetry. **Source fragment:** “The DSC curve in the second heating cycle is presented”.

**Structure (editorial schematic, not a quotation):** [sample + was heated ... cooled ... reheated] -> [specified-cycle curve + is presented + extraction purpose]. Past procedure and present reference to the displayed curve have different jobs.

**How it works:** A method paragraph gives chronological thermal steps before naming the trace used for extraction. Past passive focuses the sample and procedure; the definite second cycle resolves which measurement produced the reported property.

**Original application:** The sample was heated to 140 °C, cooled to −30 °C, and reheated at 5 °C/min. The transition temperature was determined from the second heating trace.

**Limits:** These are invented conditions. Retain the supplied ramps, holds, atmosphere, cycle and extraction convention; do not add an unstated criterion for the transition.

## P3 — auxiliary source

Ryoga Hojo et al. (2026), “Imidazophenothiazine-based photocatalysts enable the organocatalyzed atom-transfer radical polymerization of styrene,” Chemical Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1039/D6SC03956A); [full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13459050/). Europe PMC XML inspected; synthesis and characterization passages selected, not an endorsement of all mechanistic claims.

### P3a: Connect a synthetic modification to its intended function

**Locator:** Synthesis of an improved O-ATRP photocatalyst, final portion. **Source fragment:** “The synthesis followed a modified procedure”.

**Structure (editorial schematic, not a quotation):** [definite synthesis subject] + [followed] + [modified-procedure object], [participial phrase identifying the modification’s purpose]. Purpose does not establish the modification’s success.

**How it works:** A definite synthesis refers back to the target already introduced. A past-tense route statement is followed by the purpose of the modification. Keep the design hypothesis distinct from the reaction performed and from its measured consequence.

**Original application:** The ligand was prepared by modifying the reported coupling route to introduce a solubilizing side chain. Its effect on solubility was assessed separately.

**Limits:** A purpose clause states intent, not successful improvement. Cite the actual precursor procedure and include reproducible conditions in Methods rather than inventing them.

### P3b: Separate identity evidence from functional inference

**Locator:** same synthesis subsection, final sentence. **Source fragment:** “The purity and structure of SMAT-Ph-IPTZ were confirmed by X-ray crystallography”.

**Structure (editorial schematic, not a quotation):** [compound subject: analytical targets] + [were confirmed] + [by + method list]. The quoted clause contains the first method; other methods follow in parallel in the source. Infer only what the actual analyses support.

**How it works:** A compound subject groups the characterization targets; the passive leads into a parallel list of techniques. Attach each conclusion only to the measurements that support it. Do not let an analytical-method list imply a measured functional advantage.

**Original application:** The product was characterized by proton NMR spectroscopy and high-resolution mass spectrometry. Agreement with the expected signals supports the assigned molecular structure.

**Limits:** Use the supplied analytical results. Identity does not establish bulk purity, polymer sequence, conversion, or photocatalytic mechanism; stronger claims need their own evidence.


## P4 — core source, abstract only

Yiren Cheng et al. (2024), “Mechanically strong yet metabolizable supramolecular plastics by desalting upon phase separation,” Science 386, 875–881. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.ado1782); [author institution reproducing the abstract](https://www.tc.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/13642/). Abstract inspected 2026-10-23. Repository PDF retrieval was unreliable; body-level analysis is not claimed. Provisional editorial selection; see [attention evidence](selection-rationale.md).

### P4a: Put the exception inside the stability assertion

**Locator:** abstract, salt-bridging sentence, final relative clause. **Source fragment:** “which is stable unless electrolytes are resupplied”.

**Structure (editorial schematic, not a quotation):** [formation statement + network], [which + is + property], unless [trigger subject + passive predicate]. The relative clause describes the network; unless bounds its property.

**How it works:** A present-tense property assertion keeps its exception in the same grammatical unit. This avoids separating a favorable headline from the condition that changes its meaning.

**Original application:** The membrane retained its shape unless the bath temperature exceeded 60 °C.

**Limits:** This invented example requires measurements covering that conditional statement; a single successful test supports “retained its shape at 25 °C,” not an exhaustive unless claim. Do not infer environmental safety from dissolution or reuse the abstract's novelty adjectives as a style target.
